4. **Double conservatism (Graham 0).** FRT and PSA score 0 on a complete NAV; PLD, SPG, EQIX and AMT would also score 0 on the incomplete NAV. The market-implied cap rate is below the conservative 7.29% for 8 of 10 companies (PLD 3.75%, EQIX 4.00%, SPG 4.97%, PSA 5.09%, AMT 5.64%, FRT 6.03%, O 6.47%, EQR 6.53%); only the two office REITs (BXP 8.34%, ARE 8.71%) are above it. The single cap rate therefore penalizes low-yield asset types (industrial, data centers, towers, storage, malls) before any margin of safety and the 50% discount, so scores of 0 reflect the cap rate more than the asset quality. **Rule question:** an asset-type cap spread or a floor at the market's own implied rate; not changed here.
5. **Cap rate input.** `scripts/gov_yields.csv` holds annual averages only (the latest is 2025: 4.29%), not the current yield; the conservative cap rate is 7.29% (floor 6% not binding). A spot yield would move the cap rate by the difference between the current and average yield.
6. **NAV approximations.** Stabilized NOI is the latest fiscal-year NOI of the whole portfolio (the non-stabilized part is not separable); EQIX uses the revenue less cost-of-revenue proxy (which includes depreciation, so its NOI is understated and its NAV is conservative). Non-OP-unit noncontrolling interests are not separable: the whole NCI is deducted and the weighted diluted shares are used, which can double count where the diluted share count already includes OP units (EQR, PLD, ARE). Finance lease liabilities are added as a claim and may already sit in the debt tag. Goodwill is excluded.
