Described, not fixed. "Literal" = what the code does today. Items marked (new) appeared or changed after the round-1 changes.

**A. Ambiguous or impossible from the data**

1. **Interest expense = 0 or tag absent (Buffett 3.6 #2, Graham 3.3 #3).** XBRL cannot tell "zero" from "not retrieved". Literal: if the company has the tag in other years, the missing year is `missing`. If the tag never exists, it is treated as zero interest, which scores 3.
2. **Absent cash-flow line items are still `assumed_zero` when no proxy exists.** For acquisitions this now applies to KO (FY2023–25) and MCD (FY2021, FY2023, FY2025), where neither the cash tag nor `GoodwillAcquiredDuringPeriod` is reported. (new) The goodwill proxy is only a lower bound: MSFT's FY2024 proxy is $51bn against about $69bn paid.
3. **Total D&A for MSFT, ORCL, AVGO, NUE and INTC still comes from the derived Depreciation + AmortizationOfIntangibleAssets** (no cash-flow total tag exists in companyfacts for them). (new) MSFT FY2025 = $28.0bn against about $34.2bn in the cash-flow statement. The gap is mostly finance-lease ROU amortization (about $3.4bn, tag `FinanceLeaseRightOfUseAssetAmortization`) and "other". A company-independent rule for adding it was not found. The tag is also missing in some AVGO years (Depreciation only). D&A affects EBITDA (3.6), maintenance capex (3.5 #4) and owner earnings.
4. **Financial debt "excluding leases".** KO, HD, NUE, DOW, AVGO and KHC only expose `LongTermDebtAndCapitalLease*`, which includes finance leases. Flagged per company.
5. **EBIT for NUE and DOW** is derived as pretax + interest expense (no OperatingIncomeLoss). It includes non-operating items.
6. **Pretax income for ORCL and MCD** comes from the Domestic + Foreign components (no consolidated tag).
7. **Gross profit** is not derivable for ORCL, UBER and MCD, so those sub-items are not applicable. Where it is derived (NUE, part of DOW), "cost of revenue" is not comparable across cost structures.
8. **Graham 3.1 #2 (years since first annual report).** Only the earliest 10-K of the same CIK is used. Predecessor history (DOW/TDCC, KHC, AVGO/Avago, UBER pre-IPO) is not detected.
9. **Period-end shares outstanding (new).** The balance-sheet tag is missing in recent years for KO, MCD, NUE and DOW, so the cover-page count (a few weeks after fiscal year end) is used, flagged `shares_out_cover_page`. Splits are applied by filing date. UBER's t−4 count (FY2021) sits before the buyback programme; dilution of 6.1% is real.
10. **Median-based owner earnings (new).** For cyclical NUE the last-5 median (FY2021–25) includes the 2021–22 boom, while the previous-5 median (FY2016–20) is a trough. Growth of 26% is clipped to 12%, and fair value is $514 against a $250 price (reverse-DCF growth −0.4%). A median does not neutralise a full-cycle swing. For UBER (9 years) the R14 fallback is used and gives a turnaround, so growth is set to 0%.
11. **Stage-1 growth when the previous-5 median is ≤ 0** is clipped to 0% and flagged (same assumption as before).
12. **Graham 3.8 #4 when EPS or operating income ends at ≤ 0.** The growth is floored at −100% for the gap, so an EPS collapse can give a negative gap and 2 points. When the EPS start is ≤ 0 and operating income is positive, the gap is treated as ≥ 10pp (0 points).
13. **Tax rate (Buffett 3.4).** "Implausible" is not defined. Literal: pretax loss or a missing input → 25%. Otherwise clip to 0–40%. Many clips are caused by one-off tax items (flagged per year).
14. **Derived DPS (new).** CRM (dividends only from FY2022) and UBER get DPS = 0 by derivation, and INTC's missing years come from dividends paid / basic shares. This is a payment-per-share average, not the declared rate, so it can differ from DPS in years with mid-year share-count changes.

**B. Results that look economically odd (rule applied literally)**

15. **Price score separation is still weak.** (changed) Price score is 0 for 8 of the 12 companies. NUE (10.0) and KHC (10.0) come out high because of a cyclical median base with growth clipped up to 12% (NUE, whose latest owner earnings of $1.9bn are well below the $5.4bn median) and a 12%-growth assumption on a low-quality base (KHC), and neither is suitable to buy (KHC quality 38.7). DOW scores 1.2.
16. **Reverse-DCF implied growth (new)** is 14–24% for MSFT, ORCL, KO, MCD and HD, and 32–46% for CRM, AVGO and UBER. This is far above the 12% cap in the fair value, so the market price embeds growth the model does not allow, or a discount rate below 10%.
17. **Negative fair value** remains for INTC (−$1) with a positive median base: net debt exceeds the value of the small positive owner earnings. The price score is "not applicable".
18. **Graham Number cannot be computed** for MCD (negative equity), DOW, INTC and KHC (normalized EPS ≤ 0). Graham Tech cannot be computed for ORCL (adjusted FCF base ≤ 0, AI capex) and AVGO (real dilution of 14.8% from the VMware deal). UBER is now valued after the dilution change.
19. **ORCL fair value is $13.7 against a $141 price.** (changed) Net debt of about $98bn and stage-1 growth of 0% (median growth of −0.4% clipped up) dominate the result; the last-5 median ($11.6bn) is held up by the latest year ($19.5bn) only at the top end.
20. **MCD's ROIC (about 22%)** comes from invested capital that is positive only because of debt (negative equity). Correct per the formula, but it reflects leverage.
21. **ai_pending is ignored by coverage.** Every company shows coverage near 100%, although up to 19 of 100 Buffett points and 6 of 100 Graham points are undecided. The min–max range is the only place this shows.
22. **Graham 3.4 and 3.6 use EPS as reported.** One-off charges (KHC, DOW, INTC) drive deep drawdowns and negative start/end averages.
23. **Boundary choices.** "Below 10%" in Buffett 3.7 #3 is treated as ≤ 10%. For the Graham Tech quality cap, "70–84" is read as 70 ≤ score < 85.
24. **KO FY2025** CFO of $7.4bn is far below the other years, probably a one-off cash item (not verified). With the median base its effect is smaller (fair value $30.7 against $12.8 in round 1).

**C. Round 4 and round 5 items**

25. **Deferred revenue at cost basis (round 5; replaces the full exclusion).** Graham 3.2 falls back from the maximum for MSFT (15.0 → 13.7) and CRM (15.0 → 12.7) and ORCL (7.3 → 6.7); AVGO, ADBE and INTU stay at 15.0. The gross margin used is that fiscal year's own, not the latest year's, for every year in the 5-year consistency test (the latest year is identical). ORCL and INTU have no gross margin (no GrossProfit tag), so the 30% default applies (flag `deferred_revenue_default_30pct`). Graham 3.3 #2 now uses the same adjusted working capital for TECH_ASSET_LIGHT.
26. **Graham Tech cap is now continuous but still steep between quality 85 and 95** (0.7× of multiple per quality point). INTU (quality 93.8) gets a 24.4× cap against a 25× growth multiple, so its value (383) is almost entirely set by the cap interpolation, and a 5-point quality move would shift it by about 3.3 turns of FCF. ADBE (80.0) gets 16.8×.
27. **INTU customer funds use a proxy for FY2022–FY2026.** `FundsHeldForClients` is only reported through FY2021 (later years are extension tags). Restricted cash (0.2, 0.0, 3.5, 6.6, 4.5bn) is used as a lower bound for the funds held and the matching liability is assumed equal. The real balance-sheet amounts (which also include customer investments) are probably larger, so INTU's liquidity is still somewhat overstated or understated depending on the mix. Needs a check against the filing.
28. **Roll-forward factor.** At the 12% growth cap the base is multiplied by 1.2544 (MSFT 90.8 → 113.9bn, AVGO 14.7 → 18.4bn, ADBE 5.7 → 7.2bn). For ADBE and INTU the rolled base (7.2bn, 4.0bn) is still below the latest-year owner earnings (7.6bn, 6.6bn). Gate-failed companies keep the unrolled median.
29. **INTU gross-margin sub-items remain not applicable.** After FY2017 INTU's product and service cost lines are extension tags that companyfacts does not expose. The component rule needs both a goods and a services line, so it only applies to FY2017 (outside the window).
30. **Cash exclusion for customer funds** (round 4) found nothing to exclude in the cash figure: INTU's primary cash tag already excludes customer funds.
31. **Graham 3.7 #3** gives full points for a negative start and a positive end (INTU, flag `tbvps_first_year_nonpositive_improved`). Unit-tested; no behavior change.
32. **ROIC operating-IC fallback** (Buffett 3.4) still uses unadjusted current liabilities, so for INTU it is inconsistent with the customer-funds-adjusted current liabilities. The fallback is not triggered for INTU.
