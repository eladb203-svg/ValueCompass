### A. Definitions that could not be computed as written (inputs not extractable at the specified granularity)

1. **Liquid assets (Graham 3.2.2) - missing for all 12 companies.** The definition needs government / agency bonds, *publicly traded investment-grade* corporate bonds, listed equities and pledged / restricted assets, each once and with private placements excluded. No 10-K splits corporate bonds into public vs private AND investment grade vs not in one place (MET gives public / private for all fixed maturities: 71.6% / 28.4%, but not by rating within corporates); pledged assets are scattered over several notes. No approximation was used (it would reward non-disclosure). **Consequence: Graham 3.2 is invalid under M2 for all 12 companies** (regulatory capital alone is 10 of 15 points = 67% < 70%), so Graham ranges are widened by the whole criterion and Graham coverage is 67%-82%. Decision needed: allow a portfolio-level approximation (flagged), change the weights (e.g. regulatory capital 11, liquidity 4), or require the extraction.
2. **Complex / volatile assets (Graham 3.7.3) - missing for all 12.** Needs equities, alternatives / limited partnerships, real estate, below-investment-grade and unrated fixed income, and "any remaining Level 3 asset not already in these groups". The Level 3 remainder cannot be derived from any disclosure (Level 3 totals do not say which groups they belong to). Graham 3.7 is valid only where the investment-grade share (3.7.2) is also available; PGR and WRB (no investment-grade table found) are invalid.
3. **Investment-grade share (Graham 3.7.2)** is available for 10 of 12 (PGR and WRB missing: the filings give an average rating or only the below-investment-grade unrealized-loss table, not a full rating table). Sources differ: amounts (TRV, CB, HIG, PRU, MET, AIG), stated percentage (ALL 92.1%, CINF 97.5%), complement of a stated non-investment-grade-and-unrated share (RNR 5.0%), fair-value column of a percentage table (AFL). Unrated is counted as not investment grade where the filing shows it (AIG, RNR). CB includes fixed maturities held in Other investments.
4. **Regulatory capital multiple is missing for 5 of 12** (CB, TRV, PGR, AIG, HIG): the 10-K says only "exceeds the thresholds" / "in excess of minimum requirements" or describes the BSCR / GCC without a number (rule: "exceeds requirements" without a number = missing; as BANK D12). Seven numbers are not comparable in form: WRB 397% (lead subsidiary, ACL basis), CINF 5.6x ACL (lead subsidiary), ALL derived 490% from reported amounts (AIC), MET "in excess of 350%" (a floor, company-defined combined ratio on CAL), PRU 409% (PICA, **prior year** - FY2025 not yet filed), RNR BSCR 242% (group, **prior year**), AFL Japanese SMR 995% (Aflac Japan only; intervention level 200%, a regime not named in the spec - treated as /200). The spec's "group ratio when reported, else lead subsidiary" is satisfied for none of the seven.
5. **Reserve development (Buffett 3.6.2 / Graham 3.3.1).** No uniform tag (I20 confirmed): `LiabilityForUnpaidClaimsAndClaimsAdjustmentExpenseIncurredClaimsPriorYears` or `SupplementalInformationForPropertyCasualtyInsuranceUnderwritersPriorYearClaimsAndClaimsAdjustmentExpense` for CB, PGR, ALL, WRB, CINF, RNR, AIG and MET (MET's is small); none for TRV and HIG (extracted from the notes with quotes, 2023-2025) and none usable for PRU / AFL. The LDTI remeasurement (spec (b)) is tagged (`LiabilityForFuturePolicyBenefitRemeasurementGainLoss`) for MET, AFL, AIG, CB only; **PRU's movement tables were not extracted** (assumption update and experience variance are split over four product tables per year), so PRU's reserve deviation is missing (a company with future policy benefits >= 20% of liabilities needs component (b); the code refuses to score (a) alone). ALL lacks the claims-liability tag for FY2021-FY2023, so the beginning-of-year denominator is missing in those years and the window is partial. The XBRL sign of the LDTI element is "positive = gain" (verified against MET's income statement); the code reverses it to adverse-positive.
6. **Financial debt**: total tag (`DebtLongtermAndShorttermCombinedAmount`) exists for 5 companies; others use component chains; MET and WRB had to be extracted (derived sums from the balance sheet, quotes in the CSV). MET's collateral financing arrangement (476m) is excluded.
7. **Operating ROE / adjusted equity**: the rate-driven AOCI components are itemized only where the equity statement carries them (CB, TRV AFS only, WRB, CINF, MET, PRU, AFL, AIG, HIG); PGR and ALL use the balance-sheet AFS tag; **discount-rate remeasurement is not itemized for TRV, PGR, ALL, WRB, CINF** (flag `aoci_discount_rate_not_itemized`; their future policy benefits are small) and RNR has only an AOCI total (-13m). The AOCI figures are after tax (the pretax amount for the float adjustment was not extracted).

### B. Rule issues seen in the data (no change made; K1)

8. **Underwriting margin is structurally negative for life insurers**: MET -15.5%, PRU -41.8% (5y medians) vs P&C 2.8% (median of six), reinsurance 12.0%, composite 5.1%; AFL +6.2% is the exception because its benefits are premium-funded. The spec's definition (insurance revenue less benefits, acquisition and other insurance expenses, *before* investment income, interest credited treated as an investment expense) excludes the investment spread that funds life benefits. Result: MET and PRU score 0 of 9 on Buffett 3.5.1-3.5.2 and fail the cost-free-float condition (3.5.4 = 0). The note in the spec to "check by line-of-business distribution" is confirmed: one threshold set (<=-5% = 0, >=5% = 6) cannot serve P&C and life.
9. **Total operating margin** has little discrimination at the top: 8 of 12 are at or above the full-score point (10%); range 5.8% (MET) - 25.8% (AFL). AFL's high margin partly reflects the premium-only revenue base (Japan).
10. **Operating ROE** can be inflated by the denominator: PGR 37.5% (2025), RNR 39.6% (2023), ALL 39.6% (2025) on adjusted equity after large payouts or loss years; 5y median 6.6% (AIG) - 24.7% (RNR); one company at full score (>=20%). Operating vs reported ROE gaps are large for ALL (+10.3pp), PRU (+7.5), RNR (+6.0) and CINF (-8.1: CINF's reported ROE contains large equity-security gains).
11. **Float**: reinsurance assets on future policy benefits / policyholder account balances are not tagged (life, composite), so float is gross of reinsurance there; AIG's float falls from 279.8bn to 61.2bn (Corebridge deconsolidation) and AFL's from 98.0bn to 64.4bn (USD translation of yen liabilities, -10% CAGR in USD), so the 4-year float CAGR is not an organic growth measure for companies with disposals or large FX effects. The discount-rate adjustment changes the CAGR by up to 1.5pp (MET 2.5% -> 4.1%).
12. **Non-recurring gains** are tag-based (`RealizedInvestmentGainsLosses` / `GainLossOnInvestments` / `DebtAndEquitySecuritiesGainLoss`, plus market-risk-benefit remeasurement where tagged with the sign "positive = gain"). Equity-security fair-value changes and derivative gains are included only if they sit inside the chosen tag; the placement of MRB remeasurement (expense side in MET) is an assumption (it is removed from the underwriting result). CINF's reliance on gains (56.8% of 5y net income) shows the effect of equity fair-value changes.
13. **Other revenue** (revenue not in premiums / policy fees / investment income / realized gains, e.g. fee businesses, service revenue) is not insurance revenue but its expenses are inside the total-expense identity, so the underwriting result is understated for companies with material fee revenue (share per company in the diagnostics table). Policy fees are mapped from `InsuranceCommissionsAndFees` (MET, PRU, HIG, WRB, CINF); absent for the others (flag).
14. **Catastrophe flag threshold**: "material" is not defined; 5% of premiums earned was used (interpretation): CB 5.3%, TRV 8.4%, ALL 8.1% flagged. Not extracted for CINF, RNR (and not applicable-type for MET, PRU, AFL).
15. **ALM disclosure flag**: found by pattern for 10 of 12; ALL and CINF "no match" means no sentence on liability matching was found by pattern, not that none exists.
16. **IFRS 17 / non-US filers** are outside this round (US GAAP only); the segment-sum convention for the claims liability is not needed for these insurers.

### C. Extraction feasibility (S5) - summary

Extracted with quotes (insurance_regulatory.csv, 55 rows; 53 verified - the 2 unverified rows are the ALM "no match" results for ALL and CINF): regulatory capital (7 numbers, 5 documented absences), investment-grade share (10), catastrophe losses (7), ALM presence (12), debt (2), reserve development (TRV 3 years, HIG 3 years). **Not extracted in this round (AI-extraction needed): liquid-asset components, complex-asset components, pledged assets, PRU LDTI movement tables, ALL claims-liability balances for FY2021-FY2023, IG tables for PGR and WRB, catastrophe losses for CINF and RNR.** The inline-XBRL equity statement (dimensional facts, not in `companyfacts`) was the only practical source for the AOCI components and worked for 9 of 12.
