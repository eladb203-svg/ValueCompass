Scores changed only marginally from Q1 (ALL, CINF, AIG, HIG +0.05 to +0.7 points on 3.5.1-3.5.2). Needs a K1 decision, not a mapping fix.
2. **Accretion source and comparability.** All accretion amounts come from tagged LDTI roll-forward elements in the inline XBRL (dimensioned, not in `companyfacts`); no AI extraction with a quote was needed. Aggregation (company total member, aggregate context, or sum of product contexts) is recorded per company-year. MET's amounts are the sum of the five product / segment roll-forwards it tags (5.2bn on future policy benefits of about 209bn); other long-duration blocks disclosed elsewhere are not included, so the adjustment may be understated. **AIG's series is not on one basis:** FY2021-FY2022 (original presentation with Corebridge consolidated, accretion 2.3-2.5bn) vs FY2023-FY2024 (small, after Corebridge became a discontinued operation / was deconsolidated); no roll-forward for FY2025. The deferred-profit-liability accretion (PRU 0.6bn, others) is excluded.
3. **Q2 liquidity discriminates little.** By balance-sheet category (all fixed maturities and equities count as liquid) 9 of 12 companies are at the full score (>= 80%); range 72.1% (MET) to 100.0% (PGR); median 87.3%. Credit quality and duration are not part of the measure (Graham 3.7.2 covers credit; ALM is a flag). Pledged / restricted assets are not deducted (flag only). Complex / volatile assets do discriminate (4.6% PGR to 42.0% CINF; 1 at full, 3 at zero) but listed equities are counted as liquid **and** as complex, so a company such as CINF (12.7bn of equities in 31.8bn) is penalized on the second while scoring full on the first.
