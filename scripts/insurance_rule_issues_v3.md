### Open issues after round 3 (no threshold changed; K1)

1. **Q6 effect.** Buffett moves: MET 44.3 -> 54.9 (+10.6) and PRU 48.7 -> 57.3 (+8.6) (both flagged, > 3 points); TRV +1.7, WRB +1.8, PGR +1.5, RNR +0.8, ALL +1.4; CB, CINF, AFL, AIG, HIG unchanged. No rank changes in either model; Graham is unchanged (no Q6 item). Cost of float puts the lines on one scale: 5-year median cost -6.9% (CB) ... -0.6% (ALL) for P&C / reinsurance / composite, and +0.7% (MET) / +1.6% (PRU) for the two spread-product life insurers, AFL -4.3%. Life is no longer structurally at zero: MET 4.87 of 6 and PRU 3.22 of 6 on 3.5.1.
2. **Little discrimination at the top.** Cost <= 0 is the full score regardless of how negative it is: 9 of 12 companies are at 6 of 6 on 3.5.1 (and 10 of 12 at 3 of 3 on 3.5.2: MET also has all five years below the yield, PRU 3.8 of 5). The scale separates only companies whose float costs money (MET, PRU). A float that earns -7% (CB) scores the same as one that earns -0.6%.
3. **ALL loses the whole cost-of-float measure.** Its float is incomplete in FY2021-FY2023 (claims-liability tag missing) and the components change again in FY2025, so only FY2024 has a comparable average float: 3.5.1, 3.5.2 and 3.5.4 are missing, Buffett 3.5 becomes invalid under M2 (3.7 of 15 observed) and Buffett coverage falls from 100% to 85% (status still full). In round 2 ALL scored on the underwriting margin. The inputs would come from the claims-liability balances in the reserve tables (AI extraction).
4. **Comparability breaks remove single years** (a float component starts / stops being tagged between the two year-ends): CB FY2021, PRU FY2023, AFL FY2022, AIG FY2024, ALL (four years). Each of those companies still has >= 3 years. Items are scaled from the known years (partial window flagged).
5. **AIG basis mismatch.** FY2023 numerator comes from the restated income statement (Corebridge as a discontinued operation) while the float at the end of 2023 still contains Corebridge's liabilities (average float 247bn): AIG's FY2023 cost of float (-3.3%) is understated. FY2022 (original basis) is consistent; FY2025 is on the new basis (float 60bn). The deconsolidation year FY2024 is excluded by the component rule.
6. **Currency.** All 12 sets of statements are in USD; the USD 10-year Treasury yield is used for 11 companies and the JGB 10-year yield (0.07% in 2021 ... 1.54% in 2025) for AFL as instructed. MET, PRU, AIG and CB hold material non-USD float (Japan, Europe, Asia) but are compared with the USD yield; with a yen / euro mix their hurdle would be lower, which would only worsen MET and PRU (cost already close to the yield in 2021 for PRU: cost 3.0% vs 1.45%). Not computed: it needs the currency split of the float.
7. **Yield scale for non-positive yields.** The rule 'cost >= yield = 0' is undefined when the yield is zero or negative (JGB 2016 and 2019): implemented as a positive cost counting as above the yield (ratio 2.0, flagged) and a non-positive cost as below. No year in the 2021-2025 scoring window is affected; the rule is covered by a test.
8. **Cumulative condition (3.5.4).** The condition compares the sum of yearly costs with the sum of yearly yields (rates, not amounts). All 11 companies with enough years PASS; the float-growth points then depend only on the valid-year CAGR: AFL 0 (CAGR -6.4%, yen translation), AIG 0 (-5.9%), MET 1.64 and PRU 1.59 (partial scale between 0% and 5%).
9. **Float definition carried over.** Float is net of reinsurance only where the reinsurance recoverable is tagged (not for life policy liabilities), excludes the discount-rate remeasurement using the after-tax AOCI figure, and includes unearned premiums net of prepaid reinsurance. The cost of float uses the underwriting result after the Q1 accretion adjustment, so life insurers' benefits exclude the interest accretion.
10. **Distribution check (K1).** Cost of float by line: P&C median -2.4% (ALL's single comparable year, -0.6%, is shown but not scored), reinsurance -5.2%, composite -2.6%, life +0.7% (MET) / +1.6% (PRU) / -4.3% (AFL); all 12: min -6.9%, p25 -3.9%, median -2.6%, p75 -1.1%, max +1.6%.
